Muzora is a small Android client focused on random listening from your Navidrome or any Subsonic-compatible server.

Features:
• Shuffle queue with prefetch and offline playback from a local cache
• Likes, dislikes, favorites, and library search
• Internet radio (server stations + custom streams) with ICY metadata
• Guest radio before login
• English and Russian UI

Connect to your own server URL — credentials stay on the device and are only sent to the server you configure. No accounts, ads, or tracking in the app.

Cleartext HTTP is allowed so you can reach LAN servers (http://192.168.x.x) and radio streams that are still HTTP. Prefer HTTPS when available.
